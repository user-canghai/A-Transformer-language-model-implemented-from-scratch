Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to collect pretty rocks. She would collect them all day long. One day, she found a big, shiny rock. It was very pretty. Lily wanted to keep it.
Lily took the rock home and showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Be careful, it is not so dirty." Lily put the rock in the special place. She thought about what she could do with her collection.
That night, Lily met a nice doctor. The doctor was nice and gave Lily a big hug. Lily was happy and went back to her mom. She showed her mom the pretty rock and said, "Look, I found a pretty rock!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Lily! You are very brave and smart."
