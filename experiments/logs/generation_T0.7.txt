Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a small toy named Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear was a reliable friend. He always helped Lucy when she needed it.
One day, Lucy and Mr. Bear went to the park to play. They played on the swings and slide. Mr. Bear was very happy to play with Lucy. They had so much fun.
But then, Mr. Bear started to talk! "I'm Mr. Bear," said Lucy. Mr. Bear looked at Lucy and said, "I don't know you! I just want to play with you!" Lucy and Mr. Bear played all day, but Mr. Bear just wanted to play.
One day, Lucy and Mr. Bear went to the park. They played catch and had lots of fun. Lucy was happy that Mr. Bear was gone. She knew that Mr. Bear was not a good friend. She was a good friend to all.
